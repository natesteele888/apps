# Tests for the family sports apps

Browser tests (Playwright + Google Chrome) that drive the real pages at phone size. No data, no accounts: fixtures use placeholder names only.

```
NODE_PATH=/path/to/node_modules tests/run.sh           # everything (about 20 minutes)
NODE_PATH=/path/to/node_modules tests/run.sh locker    # one app
```

- `health`: the shared health check and problem report (`health.js`) that every app loads: a healthy phone, crash and failed-promise logging that survives a reload with repeats folded, the no-save bar, the Something wrong? sheet sending through the share sheet or copying, installed detection, and a check that every app loads the script and has the button.
- `numbers`: the anonymous app counts in `install.js` (served on its real https address), the "This is me" mark (`?nateme`, `toolbox/me.html`) that stops a browser counting and sends its code to `app_me`, and the numbers page (`toolbox/numbers.html`) leaving marked phones out and flagging new phones on the LYBS Dashboard and Ballclub until Got it.
- `picks`: Gameday Picks' core (slate keys for the NFL week and the MLB/NBA/NHL day, local dates, reading ESPN's scoreboard, records and team stats per league or all), then the page with Firebase and ESPN stubbed: the four league tabs, this week versus today, picks saved under a league-dated slate, locking at game time, the per-league and all-leagues boards, a 320 px phone.
- `photoblueprint`: the sheet math (angle of view, shutter and aperture formats, APEX, compass, the field-of-view wedge, share links), the EXIF reader on JPEGs built in the test (both byte orders, GPS, damaged files), then the app: every screen at phone size, a photo whose camera notes fill the sheet and drop the pin, stance and direction, the blueprint sheet, share links, edit, delete, backup, themes and units, small phones, the Back button, offline with the real service worker.
- `eightcount-core`: the pure timing math (beats, lock-in marks, share links).
- `eightcount`, `eightcount-more`: the screens, click timing (checked with an oscillator spy), lock-in across two pages with a shifted clock, recording with a fake camera, offline.
- `playboard`: every sport, drag by mouse and touch, paths, animation, share links, picture and print, offline.
- `locker`, `locker-back`: hand-checked stat math, streaks, pitch rest days, backup and restore, the card picture, the Back button.
- `practiceclock`: block and station schedule math across pauses, sleep and skips, what a linked watch gets (the `pk` field: names, lengths and the clock numbers, never notes), the runner on a fake clock, share links, print, backup.
- `read20`: the story library (levels, page lengths), day, streak and month math, damaged saves, tap-to-highlight, the nightly timer on a fake clock across two stories, idle pause, the log, offline.
- `read20` also covers readers (several children, migration, removal), the older-readers library and theme, family links, and one-reader links for a child's own phone (the phone only ever touches that reader's record).
- `read20` also covers the watch link (`watchlink.js`, Firestore REST stubbed): nothing is sent until a watch is linked, a reader is picked for the watch, the watch's reading sessions land once in that reader's log (odd ones and other apps' entries skipped, never doubled by later syncs or a reload), the goal and streak follow, the summary the watch shows (only the `r20` field, only when it changed), the party when the goal is reached on the watch, and unlinking.
- `getready` also covers the watch link: the lists and recent check marks the watch gets (no photos), and watch ticks landing unless the phone changed that day later.
- `picks` also covers the watch link: the watch gets the player and the NFL standings (top 8), never picks.
- `getready`: checklist and day-complete logic, streaks that skip days off, reminder windows, the calendar file, dance poses, then the app: ticking items, the full-screen dance, add/rename/reorder/remove, timers on a fake clock, photos, reminders, the log, your own GIFs, small-phone layout, offline, and the Google backup against a stubbed Firebase (merge rules, two phones, photos up and down, sign out, blocked account).
- `catchlog`: Catch Log with Firebase stubbed: the sign-in gate, starting a crew (one document with a long random code), adding an angler, a trip with a new spot and a photo (the photo is its own document), trophies, fixing and removing a catch, the spots map (real Leaflet when `LEAFLET` points at a folder with leaflet.js, otherwise it must fail gracefully), a second phone joining by the crew link, and a crew the account can't open.
- `whoswhere`: the clash engine (kids, drivers, drive time, midnight, the November clock change), repeats, .ics parsed back, week text.
- `tournamentpack`: 18 hand-worked standings cases with every tiebreaker, brackets of 4, 6 and 8, .ics, the packing list.
- `walkup`: the mix plan and ducking math, the voice polish chain rendered offline and measured, fake-microphone recording, the song library, backup with audio.
- `walkup-lineup`: positions by inning (cleaning saved grids, the per-inning check for a position twice or nobody, Copy inning 1 to all), the Team tab editor, and the per-inning rows the Dugout watch gets through the bridge.
- `walkup-requests`: asking a parent for a song (request links, the parent page with a stubbed share sheet, file names that carry a code, matching a received file to its player, the Android share target through the real service worker, audio formats: real WAV, MP3 and webm decodes plus a name-and-type table for the rest).
- `scoreboard`: the game engine (baseball count and innings, basketball fouls and bonus, football downs, clock math, undo all the way back) and the board screens.
- `fairway`: the golf core (to-par math, the leaderboard and ties, groups starting on 10, cleaning, closest-to-the-pin distances and averaging), then the page with Firebase blocked: the demo board, field, score, course and a flipping player card, starting a tournament on the phone, a closest-to-the-pin hole measured with the browser's made-up GPS, the sound switch and names shown as text. Phase 1: who's in and making it official, the snake draft and team board, match play, tee times and the calendar file, chat and polls in core; on the page, getting a tournament going on one phone through official, chat, a tee time and the calendar download. Games: each golfer's 3 by 3 bingo card, birdies marking themselves and three in a row in core; on the page, tapping a line on the Games tab. Side games: skins carry-overs, Nassau, wolf, the snake, greenies, a for-fun stake and the fewest payments that settle it, all netting to zero, in core; on the page, picking games, marking a three-putt and seeing who owes who. Hole review: the plain line, the next hole from your tees (a start on 10 included), cleaning, and Jack noticing only real patterns in core; on the page, the review showing once a score is in and its taps kept on the phone, not in the shared tournament. Jack: Clean by default, the kid lock, the adult check and earbuds for Super crude, swears dropping out at Clean, and notes per course and hole in core; on the page, saving a caddie note that stays on the phone and the language settings. Recap and season race: nothing until the cards are in, the champion, low round and worst hole, a recap that fits one chat message, and season points by finish across this year only, in core; on the page, the recap on the Board, sharing it in the chat, and you marked in the season race. My bag: typical yards until set, the shortest club that gets there and a club named on par 3s in core; on the page, saving the bag and the next par 3 naming a club. Hole map: cleaning, GPS yards to the green, tap-to-measure scaled by GPS or the scorecard and your GPS spot on the picture in core; on the page, adding a picture, setting the tee and green, measuring, marking the green with the made-up GPS and nothing reaching the shared tournament. Live sharing between phones is not covered.
- `filmlite`: frame stepping, loops, tags, drawing and angle math, a video recorded in the page, notes that come back for the same file.
- `situations`: Where Do I Go? runs LYBS Ballclub's own trainer, so this serves `lybs-ballclub` beside this repo (next to it, or `BALLCLUB=/path`) and checks the field draws, a ball plays, Fundamentals opens in the store page's info sheet, and it opens offline after one visit. It also covers the org settings ("org in a box", `situations/org.js`): LYBS stays the unchanged default, a league file (`?org=sample`) sets the name, logo, colors and teams, the phone remembers it, the setup screen's share link sets up another phone, and a league can point at its own play files. CI checks out `lybs-ballclub` for this job.
- `secretary`: Meeting Secretary with six demo members, one absent. Checks the Unanimous button counts only who is present (5–0–0, not 6–0–0), a Nay gives 4–1–0 and is never called unanimous, an Abstain gives 4–0–1, the minutes' attendance and quorum lines, and the closing line has no stray comma when no secretary is picked.
- `snackshack`: Snack Shack Screen's folder-name rules (times, days, dates and ranges over the new year, seasons, game days, parked folders) and the loop order (sponsors and the menu taking turns), then the TV page with the Drive feed stubbed: sample slides with no feed, every playable file saved once (parked and too-big files skipped), what plays at noon versus the evening, the Today at the field card, playing from saved copies with the internet down, and the setup page checking the web app URL and handing back the screen link.
- `live-smoke.cjs <app> <text>`: opens the published page and checks the service worker, manifest icons, offline reload and console errors.

Not covered by any test (needs real phones): real microphones and Bluetooth speakers, the iPhone silent switch, real wake locks and vibration, two real phones' clocks lining up, real music and Bluetooth speaker delay, iPhone Safari recording, real touch hardware.
