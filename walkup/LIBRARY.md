# Walk-Up song library: a guide for board members

Walk-Up can get a whole batting order ready in one tap: every player gets the ballpark announcer voice and a song. This page is how the league keeps the songs that make that work. No coding is needed, and nothing here changes from year to year except the songs.

## How one tap works

```
 Ballclub lineup ──┐                      ┌── song named for the player ("Jordan - Artist - Song") goes to Jordan
 or typed / pasted ├─► Get lineup ready ──┼── everyone else shares the other library songs, unused ones first
                   │                      ├── no songs on the phone yet: built-in sounds, so Play still works
                   │                      └── anyone with no voice gets the announcer voice ("Now batting. Number 7. Jordan!")
                   └─► coach taps Change next to anyone to swap a song
```

Songs a coach already picked are never replaced. Tapping again only fills the gaps.

## Where the songs live

- Keep the songs in **one private Google Drive folder owned by the league account**, shared only with that season's coaches (Viewer). Never "anyone with the link", never a public site, and **never in GitHub**: the songs are copyrighted and file names can carry kids' names.
- Use clean versions. MP3, M4A or WAV all work; MP3 keeps phones lighter.
- Name a returning player's song **`First - Artist - Song`** (or `First Last - Artist - Song` when two players share a first name). Walk-Up gives it to that player automatically. Any other name is a general library song anyone can get.

## What a coach does once a season

1. In Walk-Up, open **Songs**, tap **Add songs**, open **Drive** in the picker, go to the league folder and select all the songs. They are copied onto the phone and work with no signal.
2. Bring in the lineup: **Team > From Ballclub** (when the team uses Ballclub on this phone) or add or paste the players.
3. Tap **Get lineup ready**. Check the list, tap **Change** for anyone who wants a different song.

Each new song starts where it kicks in, not on a quiet intro. Coaches can still move it with **Trim**.

## Each new season

- Add new songs to the folder, rename any that belong to a player, and remove the folder sharing for coaches who left.
- Nothing in the app needs to change.
