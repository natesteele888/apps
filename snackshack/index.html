Snack Shack Screen

League info on a loop, fed from Google Drive

Drop pictures, short videos or a Google Slides deck in a Drive folder. The TV picks them up on its own, plays what fits the day and time, and keeps playing if the internet drops.

See it play

1Make the Drive feed one time, on a computer, about 3 minutes

  1. Go to script.google.com and click New project.
  2. Delete what's there and paste the feed script.
  3. Pick setup at the top and click Run. Allow it. It makes a Snack Shack Screen folder in your Drive with starter folders inside.
  4. Click Deploy, New deployment, the gear, Web app. Execute as Me, who has access Anyone. Deploy, then copy the Web app URL.

Nothing in your Drive gets shared. The feed only hands out files inside that one folder, so keep it to things you'd show on a public screen.

2Connect it

Open the screen

3Put it on the TV

  1. Plug a Google TV stick into the TV, and plug the stick's power into the wall (not the TV's USB), so it stays awake when the TV is off.
  2. On the stick, install Fully Kiosk Browser from the Play Store.
  3. In Fully's settings, set Start URL to the screen's link (the Google TV app on your phone has a keyboard you can paste into), then turn on Keep screen on and Launch on boot.

From then on, turn the TV on and it's already playing. Quick test with no stick: open the screen's link in Chrome on your phone and cast the tab.

What plays when

Only the folder names decide. Folders inside folders have to all match.

AlwaysAll the time (any folder with no rule word does too)
Morning, Afternoon, EveningBefore noon, noon to 5 PM, after 5 PM
5pm-8pmOnly between those times
Saturday, Weekends, WeekdaysOnly on those days
Spring, Summer, FallOnly in that season
Opening Day 2027-04-17Only that day. A range: 2027-06-04 to 2027-06-06
Sign up until 2027-03-20Up to that day, then it stops on its own
Game DayOnly on days with games (needs the league calendar in the script)
SponsorsOne sponsor after every 3 slides, taking turns
MenuThe menu comes back after every 5 slides
_Drafts, OffNever plays: a parking spot

Pictures stay up 10 seconds. Add [15s] to a file name for 15. Videos play to the end (keep them under about a minute). Number file names to set the order: 01, 02, 03.

On the TV remote: right and left skip, OK shows what's saved and when it last heard from Drive.
